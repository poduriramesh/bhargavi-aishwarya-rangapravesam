Welcome

We are delighted to invite family, friends, teachers, and well-wishers to celebrate Bhargavi and Aishwarya Poduri's Kuchipudi Rangapravesam. This milestone marks years of dedication, discipline, and passion for the classical art of Kuchipudi.

Bhargavi Poduri

Senior at Skyline High School. Bhargavi began learning Kuchipudi at the age of four under the guidance of Guru Sushma Yechuri and has pursued her dance journey with dedication for more than a decade.

Aishwarya Poduri

Freshman at Skyline High School. Aishwarya also started learning Kuchipudi at age four and has grown through years of training and performance under the guidance of Guru Sushma Yechuri.
